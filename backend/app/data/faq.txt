SHOPMIND AI E-COMMERCE FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How do I claim instant bank discounts and coupon offers?
A: Discounts are automatically applied at checkout when using select partner credit/debit cards (HDFC, ICICI, SBI) or applying valid coupon codes shown on the product details page.

Q: How long does standard delivery take?
A: Standard delivery takes between 1 to 4 business days depending on product availability and destination pincode. Express 1-day delivery is available for select eligible items.

Q: What is the return and replacement period?
A: Most products qualify for a 7-day hassle-free replacement or return window from the date of delivery. Items must be returned in original packaging with intact security seals.

Q: Are warranty and GST invoices provided?
A: Yes, every purchase includes an official manufacturer warranty (typically 1 year for electronics) and a tax GST invoice downloadable from your order dashboard.
